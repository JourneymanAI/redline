# 02: Let a visitor choose how the landing page is viewed (v2)

**Status:** open — v2, explicitly deferred past the v1 build

**What to build:** During the 2026-09-11 Impeccable surface-scope direction
round for Redline's landing page, three structurally distinct candidates
were designed against the same established Gate Board world: **Before/After
Contrast** (chosen for v1 — the same clause shown as ordinary prose, then as
a Redline flag), **Live Demo Hero** (the reranking board itself as the full
viewport, no intro copy), and **Boarding Pass First** (the check-in ticket
alone, board revealed after). A fourth, competitive challenger — **The
Timetable Rack** (severity ranked by weight/case/rule instead of only color)
— was also fused and could inform a fourth view.

Rather than discarding the three unchosen structures, evaluate building a v2
feature that lets them coexist as user-selectable views, in one of two
shapes (open which one is right until this is picked up):

- **Whole-page view switcher:** the visitor picks which of the full landing
  structures they see (a "see it differently" control), and the choice
  might persist per visitor.
- **Per-section view selection:** a finer-grained version where individual
  sections (the hero, the proof strip, etc.) each declare their own
  alternate presentations, mixed and matched rather than swapping the whole
  page at once.

**Blocked by:** None (speculative v2 work; not on the v1 critical path)
